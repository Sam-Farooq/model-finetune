# data

`intents.jsonl` is 64 short support messages across four intents.

**Every line was written by hand for this repository.** None of it was
collected from a product, exported from a help desk, scraped, or generated by a
model. No real person wrote any of these messages and no real order, invoice or
account is referred to. It exists so the training loop, the split and the
metrics have something to run on offline, and it is far too small to produce a
score that means anything about a model.

The class balance is deliberately uneven:

| Label | Examples |
|---|---|
| technical | 24 |
| billing | 18 |
| shipping | 14 |
| cancel | 8 |

That is roughly three to one between the commonest and the rarest class, which
is why the reported metric is macro F1 rather than accuracy, and why `cancel`
is the class that shows up in `ft split --report` as too thin to validate on.

`billing` and `cancel` overlap on purpose. "cancel the trial, I do not want to
be charged" is about money and about cancelling, and a bag of words cannot
separate it from "refund the annual charge". A classifier that scores well on
`shipping` and confuses those two is behaving exactly as the data should make
it behave.
